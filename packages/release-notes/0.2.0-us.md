## What's new in 0.2.0

The s6 boot now sandboxes more of its services as systemd does, keeps core
dumps, and swaps to zram. Timers follow systemd around daylight-saving changes.

- **Hardening:** rsyslog, earlyoom, udevd and the system bus run under their
  units' sandbox, read-only mounts match systemd's, and `PrivateUsers=` works.
- **Choices:** `BUS=` picks dbus-daemon or dbus-broker, `SYSLOG=` picks rsyslog
  or syslog-ng.
- **Core dumps:** kept on s6, in `/var/lib/systemd/coredump`.
- **Timers:** `OnCalendar=` around DST changes now matches
  `systemd-analyze calendar`. If you have timers in a zone with DST, some may
  fire at a different time around a change than under 0.1.0.
- **Removed:** `AUTO_MASK=`.

After upgrading, run `sudo uinitconverter init --yes` and reboot.

**Known issues:** gnome-shell may still crash at shutdown; a shutdown order fix
is included but not proven.
